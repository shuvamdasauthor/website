I am Shuvam, a media and cultural studies PhD student in the Department of Communication Arts at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. My research examines the distribution and consumption of American popular culture globally, including film, TV, games, and comics. I am especially interested in how global audiences are imagined by industry workers, and how real communities in South Asia engage with media.
